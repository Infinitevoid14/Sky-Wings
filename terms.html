Booking & fares

Fares vary by fare type (Basic, Standard, Flex) and include the baggage allowance, change policy, and refundability shown at the time of booking. All fares are quoted per passenger unless stated otherwise.

Check-in & boarding

Online check-in opens 24 hours before departure and closes 90 minutes before departure. Passengers must arrive at the gate with sufficient time before boarding closes.

Baggage
